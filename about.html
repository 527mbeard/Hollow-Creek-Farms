Our Story

Hollow Creek Farm has been in the Calloway family since 1962. What started as a small vegetable operation has grown into one of the region's most beloved agritainment destinations. Founders Bill and Margaret Calloway passed the farm to their daughter, Dana, and her husband, Rob, in 2008. Dana and Rob's kids, Tyler, Meg, and Sam, grew up on the farm and now help run it full time. The farm sits on 140 acres and includes working fields, a restored 1890s barn, a sunflower meadow, an apple orchard, and a beloved farm store stocked with fresh eggs, honey, pies, and seasonal produce. Every visit is a little different — that's the magic of farming with the seasons.
